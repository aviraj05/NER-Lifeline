Methodology & Exact Citations

  • Baseline Annual Loss (₹2,500 Cr): Extrapolated from the Assam State Disaster Management Authority (ASDMA) Post-Disaster Needs Assessment (PDNA) reports. Recent floods, for instance, submerged over 119,000 hectares of cropland and caused widespread destruction of infrastructure, leading to thousands of crores in economic deficit annually across the NER.
  • Truck Detention Costs (₹3,000/day): Derived from current Indian logistics industry standards for multi-axle commercial vehicle halting charges. Standard SLA benchmarks dictate that after a 2-4 hour free window, detention costs scale between ₹1,000 and ₹3,000 per day depending on payload type (Source: Indian Supply Chain SLA norms). We use ₹3,000/day to account for high-value/perishable delays.
  • Blockade Duration (120 Hrs): Based on historical monsoon impact data on NH6 (specifically the Sonapur Tunnel in Meghalaya), which serves as the primary artery connecting Barak Valley, Mizoram, and Tripura. Heavy precipitation frequently triggers mudslides causing continuous blockades of 4 to 5 days (120 hours).
  • Route Viability by Rainfall: Modeled using threshold indicators from the Central Water Commission (CWC) and regional terrain vulnerability assessments. Continuous precipitation exceeding 100mm drastically increases the probability of soil saturation and subsequent landslides on the steep-gradient sections of NH6.
